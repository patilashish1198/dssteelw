Self weights (N/m² of roof plan)

Roof angle, sheet weight, purlin spacing, self weight and k2 are filled automatically. Type your own value once and it stays until you clear the box (then the automatic value returns). Choosing a different sheet refills the sheet weight from IS 875 Part 1.

Load combination factors (DL, LL, WL, SL)

All four loads are separate: DL dead, LL live, WL wind, SL snow. Edit any factor and the roof, purlin and truss member tables all update. Wind is taken as computed, so suction is negative (upward) and a negative WL factor gives pressure. Defaults keep live and snow in separate cases; to make them act together, put a factor in both columns.

Truss analysis (automatic)

The app builds the truss from the span, node spacing and roof angle, finds every member's force for a 1 kN load at each top node, and scales it by the node loads. Leave a load box blank to use the app's own value, or type a value to override it.

Member design and check (IS 800:2007)

Summary

Members are grouped as tie member (bottom chord), rafter (top chord), strut (web member in compression) and sling (web member in tension). Each group uses its maximum positive force (tension) and maximum negative force (compression); bolts are counted for the governing force.

City speeds are from Appendix A of IS 875 (Part 3):1987, typed from a scanned copy, so please verify them. The 2015 edition refers to the NBC 2016 map, where some values differ. Vb is for 10 m height, terrain category 2, 50-year return, and is editable. Roof Cpe is the most severe of the four zones in Table 5, and pd = 0.6 Vz² as in the 1987 code. Ground snow is zero unless you enter it. Preliminary design only.
